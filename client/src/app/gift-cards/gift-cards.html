How to keep track of gift cards and vouchers, calmly

A gift card is a small kindness that’s easy to lose. It sits in a drawer, an inbox or an app, and by the time you remember it, it may have expired. Keeping them in one place, with their dates in view, is usually all it takes to actually use them.

Why they slip away

  • They arrive in different forms: plastic cards, emails, codes inside apps, paper vouchers.
  • Expiry dates are often in small print, and some cards have none at all.
  • There’s rarely a reminder before one runs out.
  • A single card feels too small to keep track of, so none of them get tracked.

A simple way to keep them in view

  1. Gather them once. Check your wallet, drawers, email and the apps you shop with, and note each card or voucher you find.
  2. Write down the value and the expiry date. If there’s no expiry, note that too, so you don’t have to check again later.
  3. Note where it lives. “In the kitchen drawer” or “in the coffee app” is enough. There’s no need to copy card numbers or PINs.
  4. Look at the soonest dates first. The card that expires next is the one worth planning around.
  5. Let go of the ones that have passed. An expired voucher isn’t a failure. Remove it from the list and move on.
