How to keep track of your subscriptions, calmly

Most people can name a few of their subscriptions. Fewer can name all of them, or say when each one renews. That isn’t carelessness. Subscriptions are designed to fade into the background. A little structure brings them back into view, without turning it into a chore.

Why subscriptions are easy to lose track of

  • Free trials turn into paid plans quietly, often weeks after you signed up.
  • Yearly plans renew so rarely that they’re forgotten in between.
  • Charges are spread across different cards, app stores and accounts.
  • Each amount is small enough to overlook on its own.

A simple way to see them clearly

  1. Make one list. Write down every subscription you can find: streaming, music, apps, cloud storage, memberships. A recent card or app-store statement helps you spot the ones you’ve forgotten.
  2. Note the next renewal date. It matters more than the price. Knowing a renewal is coming gives you time to decide before it happens.
  3. Put everything in the same unit. A yearly plan divided by twelve sits next to the monthly ones fairly. Added together, they give one honest number for what your subscriptions come to each month.
  4. Look once a week. A short glance at what renews in the next seven days is enough. There’s no need to check every day.
  5. Decide one at a time. When a renewal comes up, ask whether it still earns its place. Keeping it is a perfectly good answer.
