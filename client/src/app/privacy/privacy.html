Privacy, simply

Occurra is built so that what you note down stays with you. This page explains what that means in practice.

Your lists stay on your device

Your subscriptions, expenses and vouchers are stored only in your browser’s own storage (IndexedDB) on this device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no Occurra server that could receive them.

Occurra also remembers, in the same browser, when you last opened it, so Insights can mention it. That date stays on this device too.

No accounts, no profiling

  • No account and no sign-up. Occurra doesn’t know who you are.
  • No advertising, and nothing that follows you around other sites.
  • Occurra doesn’t set cookies.

Counting visits

To understand how many people visit, Occurra uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. It adds a small script from Cloudflare to each page, which records page views along with general details such as the page visited, the site you came from, and your browser, device type and country. It doesn’t use cookies, doesn’t build a profile of you, and never sees your subscriptions, expenses or vouchers.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any website host, Cloudflare handles the basic details of each page request, such as your IP address and browser, to deliver and protect the site, and may keep them briefly in logs. These requests contain only the page being loaded, never your lists. You can read Cloudflare’s privacy policy, which also covers Web Analytics.

Working offline

Occurra keeps a copy of its own files (pages, code and icons) on your device so it can open without a connection. This offline copy holds no information about you and sends nothing anywhere.

Removing your data

You can remove individual items in the app at any time. To remove everything, clear this site’s data (www.occurra.co) in your browser’s settings. Because nothing is kept anywhere else, this can’t be undone, and there is no copy we could restore.

If this ever changes

If Occurra ever offers something that would move your data off your device, such as syncing, it will be optional and this page will say so first.
