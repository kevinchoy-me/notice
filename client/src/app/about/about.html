A quiet way to notice recurring money

Subscriptions are easy to start and easy to forget. Each one is small, but together they add up, and the renewal dates tend to arrive unannounced. Occurra is a calm place to see them clearly, without budgets, scores or judgment.

What Occurra does

  • Keeps a simple list of your subscriptions: a name, an amount, monthly or yearly, and the next renewal date.
  • Shows which ones renew in the coming week, so nothing arrives as a surprise.
  • Adds them up into one gentle number: roughly what they come to each month.
  • Moves renewal dates forward on its own once they pass, so the list stays current.

A weekly moment of awareness

Occurra isn’t meant to be checked every day. Opening it once a week is enough to see what’s coming and decide, calmly, whether each subscription still earns its place. Nothing is flagged as good or bad. It simply helps you notice.

Your data stays on your device

There is no account to create and no bank to connect. Everything you enter is stored in your own browser on this device, and nothing is sent to a server. That also means it isn’t synced: clearing this site’s data in your browser removes your list.
